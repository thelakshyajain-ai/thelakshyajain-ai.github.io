Chia Seeds — A Nutrient-Dense Powerhouse Chia seeds contain a combination of omega-3 alpha-linolenic acid (ALA), dietary fiber, plant protein, calcium, magnesium and antioxidants. Their soluble fiber absorbs water and forms a gel-like substance, which can slow digestion and contribute to feelings of fullness. The ALA in chia seeds is an essential fatty acid, meaning the body cannot produce it in sufficient amounts and must obtain it from food. Chia also contains polyphenolic compounds, which contribute antioxidant activity. Its mixture of fiber, fats and protein makes it a nutrient-dense addition to a balanced vegetarian diet.
