Top 10 vegetarian superfoods which keep you should consume everyday for a better life

There are many varietes of foods in the world . Here are the top ten superfoods that you should consume everyday for a better you.

1. Chia Seeds — A Nutrient-Dense Powerhouse Chia seeds contain a combination of omega-3 alpha-linolenic acid (ALA), dietary fiber, plant protein, calcium, magnesium and antioxidants. Their soluble fiber absorbs water and forms a gel-like substance, which can slow digestion and contribute to feelings of fullness. The ALA in chia seeds is an essential fatty acid, meaning the body cannot produce it in sufficient amounts and must obtain it from food. Chia also contains polyphenolic compounds, which contribute antioxidant activity. Its mixture of fiber, fats and protein makes it a nutrient-dense addition to a balanced vegetarian diet.

2.Blueberries — A Powerful Antioxidant Blueberries contain anthocyanins, the natural pigments responsible for their deep blue-purple color. Anthocyanins are polyphenolic compounds with antioxidant activity and are being studied for their role in protecting cells from oxidative stress. Blueberries also provide vitamin C, vitamin K and dietary fiber. Their combination of fiber and naturally occurring plant compounds makes them a very nutrient dense berry.

3.Sweet Potatoes — A Nutrient-Dense Vegetarian Food Sweet potatoes are rich in complex carbohydrates, dietary fiber, potassium, vitamin C, and carotenoids. Orange-fleshed varieties are particularly high in beta-carotene, a carotenoid that the body can convert into vitamin A. Beta-carotene → Vitamin A: The body can convert beta-carotene into vitamin A, which is important for normal vision, immune function, and maintenance of skin and other tissues. Dietary fiber: Fiber supports normal digestion and can contribute to feelings of fullness. It also contributes to a healthy gut environment.Potassium: Potassium is an essential mineral involved in normal nerve signaling, muscle function and fluid balance. Vitamin C: Sweet potatoes provide vitamin C, which contributes to normal immune function, collagen formation and protection of cells from oxidative stress. Carotenoids and antioxidants: Beta-carotene and other plant compounds have antioxidant activity and help make sweet potatoes a nutrient-dense choice. Complex carbohydrates: Their carbohydrates provide the body with energy, making them useful as part of meals rather than simply treating them as a “diet food.” Interesting fact: The color matters. Orange sweet potatoes are especially rich in beta-carotene, while purple varieties contain different pigments called anthocyanins.
